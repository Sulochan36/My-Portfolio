## Vision

Taskilo is designed to solve a core problem: people create goals but fail to execute them consistently and also forget the purpose of the goal set as time passess.People deviate from thetrack they decided to move on , but several factors deviate them from the decided path or purpose.

Taskilo focuses on:

- Breaking goals into actionable tasks
- Providing timeline clarity
- Reducing friction in execution
- Retaining the purpose of setting the goal.
- Also helps in motivating
